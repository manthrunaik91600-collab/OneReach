Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

One Reach (“we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share information when you visit https://onereach.onrender.com/, contact us, or submit an enquiry through our website or an advertising lead form.

1. Information we collect

Depending on your enquiry, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, preferred service, loan or property enquiry category, preferred location, and any details you choose to provide. If you contact us through WhatsApp or another platform, that platform may also process information under its own privacy policy. Our website may collect basic technical and usage information needed to operate and improve the service.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and contact you about the service you requested.
  • To understand your requirements and connect you with a relevant service provider, bank, lender, or authorised representative where appropriate.
  • To maintain, secure and improve our website and manage enquiries.
  • To meet applicable legal requirements and prevent misuse.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share the details needed to respond to your request with relevant service providers, banks, lenders or authorised representatives, and with vendors who help us operate our website or communications. We may also disclose information where required by law. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites, platforms or service providers.

4. Loans and financial enquiries

Submitting an enquiry does not constitute a loan application or guarantee approval. Eligibility, interest rates, fees, documentation and approval are determined by the relevant bank or lender under its own terms and assessment. Please do not submit passwords, OTPs, PINs, full card details or other highly sensitive financial credentials through our forms.

5. Advertising lead forms

If you submit your information through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, we use the information to respond to the enquiry you submitted and for the purposes described in this policy. Meta may independently process your information under Meta’s own policies and terms.

6. Data retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may ask us to correct or delete personal information you have provided, or ask questions about its use. We will review and respond subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

8. Children’s privacy

Our services and enquiry forms are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Third-party links

Our website or communications may link to third-party websites or services. Their terms and privacy policies apply to their services; please review them separately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

11. Contact One Reach

For privacy questions or requests, contact One Reach through the contact/WhatsApp option on our website: https://onereach.onrender.com/.

This policy describes One Reach’s intended handling of enquiry information. Please ensure it matches your actual business practices and any applicable legal requirements.
